Plot

The Commune is a psychological horror comic about a young boy, Zixuan Liu (referred to as 407) who, after running away from his abusive homelife, finds himself trapped in a cult. The cult is led by charismatic mosquito man Anopheles, promising his cultists shelter from the horrible "outsiders" who hurt them, vowing to rid the world of evil himself.

The story has a strong focus on abusive family dynamics.
