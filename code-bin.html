Appearance

Sasha is an androgenous teenager, depicted as both lean and underweight from their treatment in the cult. They have a dyed purple curly mullet, with their natural black hair colour showing in their shaved sides. Sasha has a soft, heart shaped face, tan skin, brown eyes and thick eyebrows. They have stretched gauges, and black painted nails.

Like other cult members, they wear their uniform when in the cult. When outside of the cult, Sasha likes to wear black, purple and pink on occassion. They like wearing skirts and dresses, which they like to pair with chokers and arm warmers. Inside and outside the cult, they have to wear shock bracelets to prevent them using their powers. Sasha usually hides the shock bracelets under their sleeves and arm warmers.

Plot

The Commune is a psychological horror comic about a young boy, Zixuan Liu (referred to as 407) who, after running away from his abusive homelife, finds himself trapped in a cult. The cult is led by charismatic mosquito man Anopheles, promising his cultists shelter from the horrible "outsiders" who hurt them, vowing to rid the world of evil himself.

The story focuses on 5 focal characters. Zixuan (407), a young boy who is yet to realise the viscious cycle of abuse he's trapped in. Jeong-Hui (267), his adoptive older brother who finds himself in the cult after suffering tragedy and financial difficulty. Sasha (01), one of the first cultists, finding themselves trapped and disolusioned for most of their life, with little hope of escape. Lucas (24), Anopheles' right hand man who is as loyal as he is sadistic. And of course the man himself, Anopheles. Beloved leader and care taker, with a thrist for control and complete loyalty.
